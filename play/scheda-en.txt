TITOLO (25/30):
IDQ Biker Maps - Moto GPS

DESCRIZIONE BREVE (76/80):
Motorcycle navigator: online and offline, voice in 5 languages, road alerts.

DESCRIZIONE COMPLETA (1727/4000):
IDQ Biker Maps is the map and navigator for riders. It works online and also without internet.

ROUTES FOR MOTORCYCLES
The route is calculated for the motorcycle. You can avoid tolls, motorways and ferries and add as many intermediate stops as you like.

ONLINE AND OFFLINE
Online first: map, routes and search always up to date. If the network drops, the app switches by itself to the maps downloaded on your phone: you download Italy just once (map, routes and address search) and the OpenStreetMap maps are updated every month.

VOICE IN 5 LANGUAGES
Italian, English, French, German and Spanish, with a male or female voice. The whole app changes language.

ROAD ALERTS
Speed cameras, Tutor sections (average speed), traffic-light cameras, ZTL gates, level crossings and speed bumps. Plus reports from other users: mobile checks, accidents, traffic, closed roads and hazards. You can report with one tap too.

SPEED LIMITS
The limit on your route and your speed always in view; it turns red when you go over.

SERVICES NEAR YOU
Fuel stations (with the current price), restaurants, parking, pharmacies: with distance and opening hours.

TRACKS AND ROUTES
Record your rides, save routes and share them with address, coordinates and link, or as a GPX file.

MORE
2D or 3D map, automatic day and night theme, screen always on while navigating, small window (Picture-in-Picture), full guide and tutorial in the app.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking. Without internet (with downloaded maps) your position never leaves the phone; with online services the app sends our server only what is needed to answer, with no account or identifiers.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Always obey the traffic rules: the app is a driving aid.
